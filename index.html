Capacity Checklist

1. Nature of illness / injury

Ask:
  • “Tell me what happened today that caused 911 to be called.”
  • “What do you think is going on with you right now?”
  • “What concerns did we tell you we have?”

2. Risks of refusal

Ask:
  • “What could happen if you stay here instead of going with us?”
  • “What is the worst thing that could happen if this gets worse?”
  • “Explain back to me the risks we discussed.”

3. Alternatives

Ask:
  • “What other options do you have?”
  • “What is your plan to get medical care if you do not go by ambulance?”
  • “What will you do if your symptoms return or worsen?”

4. Rationale for refusal

Ask:
  • “Why do you not want to go to the hospital?”
  • “Knowing the risks we discussed, why are you still choosing to refuse?”
  • “What would make you change your mind and go?”
